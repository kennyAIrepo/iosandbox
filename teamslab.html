    About the hopeOS Meeting Sandbox

    A local test harness that imitates the Microsoft Teams meeting layout so hopeOS layers (holohands, props, the cross-tile ball game, the command agent) can be developed against a realistic surface. Nothing here is a Microsoft surface.

    Keyboard

    K keyboard hand (arrows move it, hold Space to cup / release to throw) · G kick off (host) · 1-9 goal seat (host) · P play/stop · M mute cues · H HoloHands (Auto → On → Off) · L HUD · hold V to talk · Ctrl+/ command popover · Esc closes dialogs. Tab: top bar → gallery (arrows between tiles) → control bar (arrows) → side panel tabs.

    Honest limits

    Holohands drawn over other people are visual only; a blind participant gets the live-region narration of game events, not hand positions. Camera play needs a camera and a tracker that reads you well; the keyboard path exists so anyone can still throw and catch, but it is a lesser experience. Captions and reactions here are mock.

    Outgoing video (C1)

    Open the More menu → Outgoing video, or add ?outgoing=1: a 1280x720 popup shows your un-mirrored camera with the hopeOS overlay. In OBS add a Window Capture of that popup, start the Virtual Camera, and pick "OBS Virtual Camera" in Teams. Pixels only: no hand packets leave this page on that path.
