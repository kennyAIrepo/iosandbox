## Design decisions this gap forces

1. **What may cross the wire.** Only HAND_STREAM 0x30 - hands 2 x 21 image + world i16, 516 B, seat number not user id (`hopeos-wire.md:62-76`) - and, only when the user turns "Body" on, pose 33 in its own packet 0x31 (198 B, 15 Hz). The 478-point face mesh, the transformation matrix and the 52 blendshapes never leave the device; the wire gap already dropped the memo's face block, and R2 makes that permanent by naming `hflags` bit 5 `FACE_EXPR` (reserved; decoder rejects bits 4-7). Reasons: metric hand landmarks and the face mesh are literally a "scan of hand or face geometry" under Illinois BIPA 740 ILCS 14/10 (`_src/ilga-074000140k10.htm`), which needs written notice and a written release before capture (s.15(b)) and a public retention/destruction policy (s.15(a)); Snap paid $35M in 2022 over Lenses on that theory although its data stayed on-device. Under GDPR the landmarks are personal data and plausibly biometric data (Art. 4(14)) but **not special category** while used for tracking rather than identification (ICO: "It only becomes this if you use it to uniquely identify someone"); the memo's local face-ID feature would be special category and is out of the pilot. Blendshapes additionally sit next to the EU AI Act Art. 5(1)(f) ban on "AI systems to infer emotions of a natural person in the areas of workplace" (applies since 2 Feb 2025) [counsel].
2. **Where the relay may live.** Landmarks travel through Microsoft-hosted Live Share (deleted within 24 h, usually 6 h; no residency statement, so a tenant-hosted Azure Fluid Relay is the documented fix), a relay in the pilot tenant's Azure subscription, or our in-memory ws relay for LAN demos only. Landmarks ride `LiveEvent` only, never `LiveState`; no relay logs or buffers payloads (R4). **New (R22):** the preferred in-Teams WebRTC path signals SDP/ICE through `LiveState`, which puts participants' IP addresses in container state for up to 24 h - use relay-only ICE and null the key after `connected`. ACS resources are created in the tenant's geography; the ACS DataChannel "doesn't provide message persistence" and is encrypted (Learn, updated 2026-03-25), so Path C3 stores nothing but identities and Monitor logs.
3. **ACS Calling SDK: self-host, do not load from jsDelivr.** The licence (`_src/acs-calling-1.46.1-LICENSE.txt` s.2) permits distributing "the object code form of the software listed in the distributables file list" - a list the package does not contain (8 files) - with three duties: add primary functionality, bind end users to protective terms, indemnify Microsoft. Self-hosting the unmodified `dist-esm/sdk.bundle.js` + LICENSE + NOTICES.txt with SRI on our origin is inside those terms and is what a tenant security review expects; `terms.html` s.4 carries the pass-through clause [counsel].
4. **Live Share licence scope.** Purpose is "specifically limited to developing applications that interact solely within the Microsoft Teams Ecosystem" (s.1(b)); dev/test in the twin is allowed under s.1(a); Live Share must never become a transport for a non-Teams hopeOS product; shipping it to the tenant means the s.3 indemnity and the no-trade-dress rule.
5. **LLM paths (voice and command).** OpenAI's `/v1/audio/transcriptions` keeps nothing by default (no abuse-monitoring or application-state retention; ZDR-eligible; no training on API data) - the voice clip exists only in flight. Anthropic deletes prompts/outputs within 30 days under the standard commercial policy, offers per-organisation ZDR, never trains on retained data without permission, and `claude-opus-5` is not a "Covered Model" (Fable/Mythos 5 require 30-day retention), so ZDR stays available as long as the model pin is not changed (R23). Names are still replaced by seat labels in the snapshot (R6).
6. **Branding of the twin.** Fluent tokens and icons are MIT, but the meeting look is Microsoft trade dress: internal harness = fine; anything shown outside the team carries the C10 "hopeOS Meeting Sandbox ... Not a Microsoft product" strip, no Teams/Microsoft logos (Microsoft's product icons "may not be used as a graphical or design element"), external name "hopeOS Meeting Sandbox", trademark footnote; real Teams screenshots only unaltered, never inside our UI, with "Used with permission from Microsoft"; manifest long name becomes "for Microsoft Teams meetings".
7. **Consent and indicators.** First-run dialog before `getUserMedia` with the BIPA notice/purpose/term/release elements (C1) and a `consentVersion` that also travels in the handshake so peers can refuse stale-consent senders; a tracking pill in every tile; a one-toggle on-device-only mode (`shareMode` in the handshake); ACS recording/transcription banners (contractual, with indemnity to Microsoft); push-to-talk only for voice; the repo's bug endpoint (public screenshots, `api/bugs.js:87-98`) is never called with a screenshot (R21).
