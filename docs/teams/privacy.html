Applies to the hopeOS Ball Pass meeting app, the hopeOS HoloHands video effect and the hopeOS meeting sandbox used in the [PILOT-PARTNER] pilot. Version [1.0], effective [DATE]. Notice version shown in the app: [consentVersion].
The short version. Your camera picture is analysed on your own device to find your hands (and, if you switch it on, your body pose). What leaves your device while you play is a small stream of hand and body landmark numbers, sent only to the other people in the same meeting so their screens can draw your hands. Face data never leaves your device. hopeOS keeps none of the landmark data after the meeting. Voice and typed commands are sent to third-party AI services only when you press the talk button or type a command.

3. The relay: how hand data reaches the other participants

Depending on how the meeting is set up, the landmark stream travels through one of: (a) Microsoft Live Share (Azure Fluid Relay), a Microsoft service for Teams meeting apps - Microsoft states that data sent through it "might be accessible for up to 24 hours, though in most cases it's deleted within six hours"; (b) an Azure relay in [PILOT-PARTNER]'s own Microsoft cloud subscription, in [GEOGRAPHY]; or (c) for engineering demos, a relay operated by hopeOS that forwards packets in memory and stores nothing. The app tells you which one is in use. When hopeOS joins a meeting as an external participant, its video and any game data travel through Azure Communication Services, which processes calls in real time and, per Microsoft, retains no call content.

4. Why we process it (purposes and legal basis)

  • Hand and body landmarks: to draw your hands and avatar on every participant's screen and run the shared ball game. Legal basis in the pilot: [PILOT-PARTNER]'s legitimate interest in running a voluntary pilot (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), with your participation always optional and an on-device-only mode available; and, where a written release is required by law (for example the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act), the agreement you give in the app's first-run notice.
  • Voice and text commands: to carry out the command you give. Legal basis: the same legitimate interest; you trigger every request yourself.
  • Technical data: to run and secure the service.

We do not use landmark data to identify or recognise you, to infer your emotions, or for any purpose other than drawing and gameplay. We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Retention and deletion (our biometric data retention policy)

  • Hand and body landmark data: not stored by hopeOS at any time. It exists in participants' browsers only while the meeting runs and is discarded frame by frame. On Microsoft Live Share it is deleted by Microsoft within 24 hours of the session. On a [PILOT-PARTNER] relay or the hopeOS demo relay it is not stored at all.
  • Face data: never leaves the device; discarded frame by frame.
  • Voice recordings and transcripts: not stored by hopeOS. OpenAI's speech-to-text endpoint does not keep the recording or the transcript after it has answered (no abuse-monitoring or application-state retention by default for this endpoint, per OpenAI's API data documentation), and OpenAI does not use API data to train its models.
  • Command text and scene lists: not stored by hopeOS. Anthropic [deletes them within 30 days under its standard commercial policy | does not store them at rest, under a zero-data-retention arrangement with hopeOS] and does not use them to train its models. [Deployment: keep the option that is actually configured.]
  • Diagnostics and server logs: [N] days.
  • Your consent record: in your browser until you clear site data or withdraw consent in the app.
  • Should a face-profile feature ever be offered (it is not part of this pilot): profiles would be stored only on your device, deleted when you unenrol, and in any case within 3 years of your last use.

Any biometric data is permanently destroyed when the purpose above is satisfied, and in every case no later than 3 years after your last interaction with hopeOS. This policy is public and applies to hopeOS and to anyone acting for it.

6. Security

Tracking runs inside your browser; no server ever sees your camera picture. Landmark packets are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS / WSS, or Microsoft's encrypted services) and are never written to disk by hopeOS. The hopeOS servers that forward voice and command requests add the service credentials and pass the request through without logging its content. Third-party code and models are hosted on the hopeOS domain with integrity checks. Access to hopeOS systems is limited to named engineers. We treat landmark data with at least the care we apply to other confidential information.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Do not share: switch on "On-device only" at any time - tracking keeps working for you, nothing is sent. Or turn your camera off.
  • Withdraw consent: in the app's settings ("Forget my consent"); this clears the record in your browser and returns the app to on-device-only mode.
  • Access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability: write to [privacy@DOMAIN] or to [PILOT-PARTNER]'s DPO. Because hopeOS stores no landmark data, there is usually nothing to hand over or delete beyond diagnostics; we will confirm within one month.
  • Complaints: you may complain to your data protection authority ([e.g. ICO, CNIL]).

8. Recordings and transcription in Teams

If the meeting organiser records or transcribes the meeting, whatever you show in your tile - including your avatar or holohands - becomes part of the organiser's recording, which is stored and governed by [PILOT-PARTNER]'s Microsoft 365 settings, not by hopeOS. The app shows a banner when recording or transcription is on.
